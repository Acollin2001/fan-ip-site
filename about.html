In one sentence

FAN/IP is a white-label fan engagement platform that gets brands, sports clubs and artists talked about without an ad budget: their fans post about them on their own social accounts, earn points and rewards, and the organisation sees how many people those posts reached and what the same views would have cost as ads.

What people call it

People look for this under different names: fan engagement platform, fan club app, loyalty program with social rewards, brand ambassador or advocacy program, UGC rewards platform, community gamification. FAN/IP covers that ground for organisations that want it under their own name, in their own colours and words.

Who it is for

  • Brands: beauty, fashion, food, coffee, sportswear and lifestyle brands, and shops.
  • Sport and esports: sports clubs, esports teams and gaming venues, and the sponsors behind them.
  • Artists and creators: bands, DJs, rappers, labels, festivals, podcasters and video creators.

What it does

  • Missions: photos, videos, posts, check-ins, quizzes, polls and predictions, in seasons, with points, levels and a leaderboard.
  • Rewards and perks: discount codes, products, early access and other perks you choose, unlocked by level or exchanged for points.
  • Review: your team approves posts before points are paid, or lets posts the network confirms through automatically.
  • Reach: views of members’ posts, and their media value at the price per 1,000 views you choose.
  • Sponsor quests: missions under a partner’s name, with a private report for the partner.
  • White-label: your name, colours, fonts and words, and your own domain from Club.
  • Four languages: English, French, German and Spanish. Works in any phone browser and installs on the home screen with notifications.

Every feature is listed on the platform page.

How it puts a value on fans’ posts

Views ÷ 1,000 × the price per 1,000 views (CPM) the brand chooses. The admin starts from €2 and the brand can set any value. The reach calculator runs the same sum on your own numbers.

What it costs

Starter: €99 a month, self-serve, 7-day free trial, up to 150 active members. Club: €490 a month. Season: €1,290 a month. Signature: from €2,900 a month. Prices in euros, excluding VAT. Fans never pay. Details on the pricing page.

Where it runs

FAN/IP is made in Cologne, Germany. Members’ data is hosted in the European Union, in Frankfurt, and processed under the GDPR. Payments go through Stripe.

Who is behind it

FAN/IP was founded by Alexis Collin, a French founder based in Cologne and a sport and esports fan. He had the idea for years, first built it for one brand, and it worked well enough that he rebuilt it so any brand, club or artist could run its own. Details are in the imprint.

Questions from artists.

More answers, for every world, in the full FAQ.

What is a fan club app for artists?

A space under your name where fans join, complete missions, earn points and unlock rewards. You write the missions and choose the rewards. Fans take part for free.

What do fans do after a show?

They post a setlist photo or a clip on Instagram or TikTok, tag the city and you, and earn points once your team approves the post. Other missions: a story for the new single, quizzes, polls, QR check-ins and inviting a friend.

How do I see which shows people talk about most?

Make one mission per show. Your admin adds up views, likes, comments, shares and media value, mission by mission from Club, and the potential reach of everyone who posted. The Ilsa Morrow demo shows a six-date tour.

How is this different from paid promotion?

Paid promotion stops when the budget does. Here the reach comes from fans posting to their own followers about a show they went to. That is word of mouth, and you thank them with rewards.

How much does it cost?

Starter is €99 a month with a 7-day free trial, for up to 150 active fans, 3 missions and 3 rewards at a time, and a leaderboard. Club, at €490 a month, suits a first tour. Season, at €1,290, is for up to 10,000 active members. Compare the plans.

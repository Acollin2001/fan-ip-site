Side by side

Why not just use what you have?

Fair answers. Some of these tools are excellent at what they do. They were not made for fans.

  • A loyalty app for your online shop

    What it does well
    Points on purchases, referral codes, a rewards page. Very good at bringing shoppers back to the checkout.
    Where FAN/IP is different
    They count what fans buy. FAN/IP counts what fans do: posts, predictions, visits, friends, seasons. Use it alongside your shop, or on its own.

  • A community platform

    What it does well
    Feeds, groups, events, courses. Very good for conversations.
    Where FAN/IP is different
    Conversation alone fades. FAN/IP gives members a reason to come back every week: missions, levels, seasons and rewards, with a chat to your team.

  • A fan-token app

    What it does well
    Tokens fans buy and vote with, rewards in a crypto wallet.
    Where FAN/IP is different
    No wallet, nothing to buy, nothing to lose. Fans earn by taking part, and your brand stays yours, not a listing among dozens of others.

  • A Discord server or a WhatsApp group

    What it does well
    Free, familiar, fast to open.
    Where FAN/IP is different
    Nothing to reward, nothing to measure, and the platform owns the space. FAN/IP runs under your name, on your domain, with your data.

  • Building it yourself

    What it does well
    Full control, if you have the team and the time.
    Where FAN/IP is different
    Missions, seasons, rewards, reviews, fair-play rules, data requests and four languages are already built and maintained. Your team works on the community, not the code.

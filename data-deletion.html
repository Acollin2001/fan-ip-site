Data deletion

Unlink an account, remove our access, or have everything deleted.

Last updated: 29 September 2026

On this page

  1. Unlink an account
  2. Remove access on each network
  3. Delete all your data

These instructions are for members of a brand’s fan program powered by FAN/IP who have linked a TikTok, Instagram or YouTube account. What we read and why is explained in our privacy policy.

Unlink an account

In the program’s app, open your profile, go to Social accounts and choose Unlink next to the account.

The data we read from that account and the access keys are deleted immediately. For TikTok and Google, we also revoke FAN/IP’s access on the network’s side.

Remove access on each network

You can also remove FAN/IP’s access directly from the network, at any time:

  • YouTube (Google): go to myaccount.google.com/permissions and remove FAN/IP.
  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services permissions and remove FAN/IP.
  • Instagram: open Settings › Website permissions › Apps and websites and remove FAN/IP.

Once access is removed, we can no longer read anything from the account.

Delete all your data

To have all your data deleted, including the posts you submitted for missions, either choose Delete my account in the program’s app, or write to contact@fan-ip.com with the program’s name and the email address you use in it.

We answer within one month. If we have reasonable doubts about who you are, we may ask for information to confirm your identity.

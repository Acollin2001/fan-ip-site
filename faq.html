What is FAN/IP?

FAN/IP is a white-label fan engagement platform. It gives a brand its own fan club, under the brand’s name: fans complete missions, earn points, move up levels and unlock rewards, and the brand sees who takes part and what they bring.

What is a fan engagement platform?

Software that gives the people who care about a brand, club or artist things to do together, and rewards them for it: missions, points, levels, rewards and a leaderboard. FAN/IP is a white-label one. The fan club carries your name, and most missions are about posting on members’ own social accounts, so you can see how many people those posts reached.

Is there a platform that rewards customers for posting about a brand?

Yes, that is what FAN/IP does. A brand gives its customers missions, such as posting a photo with the product and tagging the brand. Customers post on their own Instagram or TikTok, earn points and unlock the rewards the brand chooses. The brand sees every post, how many people it reached and what the same views would have cost as ads. Starter costs €99 a month, with a 7-day free trial.

How is FAN/IP different from a loyalty program?

A loyalty program mostly rewards purchases. FAN/IP rewards what fans do for you away from the till: posting about you, bringing a friend, coming to an event, answering a quiz. It counts the reach of those posts and puts a value on it in euros (views ÷ 1,000 × the price per 1,000 views you choose). You can run it next to the loyalty program you already have.

How is it different from an influencer or ambassador platform?

Influencer platforms help you find and pay a few creators with large audiences. FAN/IP works with the people who already like you, often hundreds of them, each with an ordinary audience of friends who trust them. Members earn points and the rewards you choose, and you see the combined reach of everything they post.

How can a sports club show sponsors what its supporters’ posts are worth?

With a sponsor quest: a set of missions under the sponsor’s name, such as matchday photos. Supporters post, and the sponsor gets a private report it opens on its phone: how many supporters took part, how many posts and views, and what that reach would have cost as ads at the price per 1,000 views you set. The Aldermoor demo shows one for a fictional club.

How can a small brand get talked about without an ad budget?

Give the customers who already like you a reason to post, and thank them for it. With FAN/IP Starter (€99 a month, up to 150 active members, 7-day free trial) you set up your own fan club online, add a few missions, such as a photo of the product in use, and choose rewards like a discount code or early access. Members climb a leaderboard as they earn points. Then share the link with your customers. The reach calculator estimates what their posts could be worth.

How much does a white-label fan club app cost?

With FAN/IP, from €99 a month on Starter, with no set-up fee. Club is €490 a month, Season €1,290 and Signature from €2,900, excluding VAT, with your own domain and set-up help from Club. Fans never pay anything. See pricing.

Who is it for?

Brands and organisations with people who care about them: consumer brands (beauty, fashion, food, lifestyle), sports clubs, gaming venues and esports teams, and artists, labels and festivals. There is a page for each: brands, sport and esports and artists and creators.

How does it work?

It works in three steps. You create missions, for example “post your unboxing and tag the brand”. Fans take part and earn points. Your team approves their posts, hands out rewards and follows the results.

What does white-label mean here?

Fans see your brand, not ours. Each brand gets its own space with its own name, colours, fonts, words, missions and rewards. FAN/IP runs behind it as one platform, and each brand's members and data stay in its own space.

What can fans do?

Depending on the plan: complete missions (photo, video or text), answer the weekly question, complete collectibles, join community goals and team battles, climb levels and seasons, appear on the leaderboard and redeem rewards in the shop.

Who checks what fans post?

Your team, or your moderators from Club. Member content submitted for a mission is reviewed before points are awarded, unless you switch on automatic approval for posts the network itself confirms, so every point is tied to a real action on a real mission.

What does the brand get out of it?

A record of what your community does: who took part, in which mission, and whether it was approved. That adds up to participation, retention, advocacy, UGC, fan insights and organic reach. Client results stay private; a demo shows the numbers on demo data, and Post impact shows yours from the first post.

What is earned media value?

The value of the attention a brand gets without paying for it, such as posts by its fans. FAN/IP prices the views of members’ posts at what the same views would have cost as ads. More definitions are in the glossary.

How do you calculate what fans’ posts are worth?

Views ÷ 1,000 × CPM, the price of 1,000 ad views in your niche. A post seen 900 times at a €6 CPM is worth €5.40. FAN/IP starts from €7 for sport, €5 for music and creators, €8 for brands and €6 otherwise, and you can set your own rate. Try it with your numbers in the reach calculator.

How many views does a fan’s post get?

It depends on the person: some fans get a few hundred views, a fan with a big following gets far more. Estimates on this site start from 900 views a post on average. Inside FAN/IP, each post’s real views are read from the member’s linked account.

How much does it cost?

Starter: €99 a month or €990 a year, no set-up fee, up to 150 active members. Club: €490 a month, set-up €1,250, up to 1,500 active members. Season: €1,290 a month, set-up €2,500, up to 10,000 active members. Signature: from €2,900 a month, set-up from €5,000, for larger communities. Prices are in euros, excluding VAT. Club, Season and Signature are billed monthly on a 12-month term. See pricing.

What is an active member?

Someone who did something in your community this month. Dormant sign-ups are not counted.

How do I see it?

Book a demo: a 30-minute video call where we show FAN/IP live, on demo data. You can also write to contact@fan-ip.com.

How do fans sign in?

In their browser, on your space's address: a FAN/IP subdomain, or your own domain from Club. They join with their email and a password, through open sign-up or an invite code you give them, and reset their password themselves. There is nothing to download.

Which social accounts can fans link?

Fans link the account they post from before sending a mission post, through each network's official sign-in, and a post link counts once, for that account. That is how their posts and views are counted. They can link TikTok, Instagram or YouTube. Instagram needs a free Creator or Business account to link; TikTok works with any account; YouTube needs a channel (free to create).

Is there a phone app?

There is nothing to download from an app store. Your space works in any phone browser, and members can add it to their home screen like an app and get notifications.

How long does a launch take?

Set-up starts on the sign-up page in five steps (brand, look, modules, plan, account), then a five-item launch checklist in your admin: logo and colours, first missions, first rewards, open sign-up, share the link. On Starter you create your space online, follow the online guide and get short set-up emails during your first weeks. From Club we set it up with you, and Season adds a design workshop for your mission calendar, levels and rewards.

Where is the data hosted?

In the European Union, in Frankfurt, Germany. FAN/IP processes your members' data on your behalf, under the GDPR.

Who owns the data?

You do: your members' data belongs to your brand. From Club you can export your members at any time. Members can also download their own data or ask for deletion from their profile, and you erase it in one click.

Can I try it for free?

Yes, on Starter: a 7-day free trial. You sign up online, pick your colours and fonts, and your space is ready. A card is asked at sign-up and nothing is charged during the trial; you can cancel before it ends. For Club, Season and Signature, book a demo.

Can I cancel?

Starter has no commitment: cancel at any time and it runs until the end of the period already paid. Club, Season and Signature are billed monthly on a 12-month term, renewed for another 12 months unless cancelled at least 3 months before the term ends, or month-to-month for 20% more with one month’s notice.

How do I pay?

By card or SEPA direct debit, through our payment partner Stripe. You add your VAT number there, download your invoices and change your card yourself.

What happens if a payment fails?

Nothing changes for your members at first: we email you and Stripe tries again for 14 days while members keep playing. If it is still unpaid, access to your admin is blocked and members can play for 15 more days. After that the subscription can be ended; you can get your data back first, and it is then deleted as the data processing agreement sets out.

What happens if we grow past our plan?

Members who already joined are never blocked. Starter caps active members at 150 a month: once reached, new sign-ups wait until next month or until you move up a plan. Club's 1,500 and Season's 10,000 are soft limits: nobody waits, and we talk if you get close. No surprise invoices.

Do fans pay anything?

No. Fans take part for free. There are no tokens or crypto, and points cannot be bought: they are earned by taking part.

Which languages does it speak?

English, French, German and Spanish. Each member sees your space in their own language, and you choose your default one. You can write missions and rewards in each language.

Can we use our own domain?

Yes, from Club: for example club.yourbrand.com. You add two DNS records shown in your admin and the domain switches on by itself once it is verified. Starter runs on a FAN/IP subdomain.

How do you stop fake posts and cheating?

Your team reviews posts before points are paid, daily limits stop spam, and fair-play rules flag suspicious activity for review. A post link counts once, for the account that posted it; anyone else who sends it is refused and flagged. No device fingerprinting, no covert tracking, no automatic bans: a person decides. From Club you can give the review work to moderators.

Does it work with our online shop or email tool, like Shopify or Klaviyo?

Yes, alongside them, with nothing to install. FAN/IP creates the discount codes your members win: you export them, add them to your online shop (Shopify or any other that takes discount codes) and mark them active, and members see and use them. From Club you can export your members to a spreadsheet for your email tool, such as Klaviyo or Brevo. On Season we set this up with you. Connections that sync on their own with e-commerce, CRM, email or ticketing tools are built on Signature and quoted separately.

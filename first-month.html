Your first month

From the day your space is created to the end of your first season: what you do, what FAN/IP does, and what changes with your plan.

On this page

  1. Day 1: your space
  2. Week 1: first fans, first posts
  3. Month 1: first figures
  4. Season end: ranking and rewards
  5. Where to start

Day 1: your space is created

You do
Go through the five sign-up steps: brand, look, modules, plan and account. Say what you are (a brand, a club or team, an artist or creator), choose your space’s name and address, add your logo, colours and fonts. Then set your password and two-step sign-in with an authenticator app: it is required for every administrator.
FAN/IP does
Creates your space under your name, ready at once. It starts with words and ready-made missions for your world: the first, a post about you, is live from day one; the others wait as drafts for you to edit and publish. Your admin opens on a six-item launch checklist: logo and colours, first missions, first rewards, your legal details, open sign-up, share the link.
By plan
  • Starter: you sign up online and set it up yourself, with the online guide and short set-up emails during your first weeks. 7-day free trial: a card is asked at sign-up and nothing is charged during the trial. Your space runs on a FAN/IP subdomain.
  • Club, Season and Signature: we set it up with you; that is what the set-up fee pays for. Your own domain, such as club.yourbrand.com: you add two DNS records shown in your admin and it switches on once verified.
  • Season adds a design workshop for your mission calendar, levels and rewards. Signature adds a member layout designed for your brand, and several brands or countries on one contract.

Week 1: first fans, first posts

You do
Share your link: post it on your social accounts, or print the QR code from your admin for the counter, the stand or the stadium. Fans join in their browser, with their email and a password, through open sign-up or an invite code you give them. Then review the first posts: approve and pay, approve only, ask for changes or reject, with a note the member reads.
FAN/IP does
Keeps every point tied to a real action: posts are reviewed before points are paid, a post link counts once, for the account that posted it, daily limits stop spam, and fair-play rules flag suspicious activity for a person to review. Members can add your space to their phone’s home screen, like an app.
By plan
  • Starter: 1 team seat, so you review posts yourself. Missions: photo or video, poll and quiz, with 3 missions, 3 rewards and 3 levels at a time.
  • Club: 3 team seats, with moderators who review posts and answers. All mission types, with no limit on active missions or rewards.
  • Season and Signature: unlimited team seats.

Month 1: first figures

You do
Open Results and take a first look: who took part, in which mission, and what was approved. Check the price per 1,000 views (CPM) used for your media value: it starts from a typical price for your niche, and you can set your own.
FAN/IP does
Reads the views of each approved post from the network and puts a value on them: views ÷ 1,000 × CPM, what the same views would have cost as ads. It adds them up by mission and over time, next to your active members, check-ins, referrals and rewards. How media value is worked out.
By plan
  • Starter: key figures. Up to 150 active members: once reached, new sign-ups join a waiting list and come in on the 1st of the month when a place is free, or as soon as you move up a plan. Upgrade to Club at any time, with set-up at €500 instead of €1,250.
  • Club: detailed results, charts and CSV export, and member export.
  • Season: an engagement review with your team every quarter. Signature: a results review every month.

Season end: ranking, rewards, season 2

You do
Review the last posts, then close the season from your admin. Hand out the rewards: discount codes are created by final rank, and you export them and add them to your online shop. Then open season 2 with new missions.
FAN/IP does
Ranks members by season XP and gives each one a personal season wrap, made to be shared. Season XP starts again for season 2; the points members earned to spend on rewards are never reset.
By plan
  • Starter: the leaderboard and the season wrap are included.
  • Club: adds badges, streaks and sprints, and as many levels per season as you like.
  • Season and Signature: add community goals and team battles.

Where to start

On Starter, from €99 a month: start your 7-day free trial and set it up yourself today. For Club, Season or Signature: book a demo, and we set it up with you. Plans and prices are on Pricing; more answers in the FAQ.

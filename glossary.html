Glossary

The words of fan programs and earned media, each in a sentence or two, with the sums FAN/IP uses.

Earned media value

The value of the attention a brand gets without paying for it: posts by fans, customers or supporters, word of mouth, press. FAN/IP puts a price on one part of it, the views of posts your members make, by asking what the same views would have cost as ads.

Media value of a fan post

What the views of one post would have cost as ads: views ÷ 1,000 × CPM. A post seen 900 times, in a niche where 1,000 ad views cost €6, is worth 900 ÷ 1,000 × €6 = €5.40. FAN/IP adds these up for each mission and in each sponsor report.

The reach calculator uses the same sum for an estimate before you start.

CPM (cost per mille)

What an advertiser pays for 1,000 views of an ad. FAN/IP starts from a typical European price for the niche, €7 for sport, €5 for music and creators, €8 for brands and €6 otherwise, and each brand can set its own rate. Change the CPM and the value moves; the views do not.

Views per post

How many times a post was seen, as the network reports it to the person who posted. FAN/IP counts views, not followers, because a follower does not see every post. Estimates on this site start from 900 views a post on average; inside the product, each post’s measured views are used.

Potential reach

The followers of the accounts that posted for a mission. It shows how many people could have seen the posts, and it is reported next to the views, never used to compute media value.

User-generated content (UGC)

Photos, videos, stories and reviews made by customers or fans rather than by the brand. In FAN/IP, UGC comes from missions: a member posts on their own account, tags the brand, and the brand’s team reviews it before points are paid.

Fan engagement platform

Software that gives the people who care about a brand, club or artist things to do together, and rewards them for it: missions, points, levels, rewards and a leaderboard. See the FAQ.

White-label fan club app

A fan club that carries the brand’s name, colours, fonts and words, while the software behind it is shared. With FAN/IP, members see your brand, not ours, and each brand’s members and data stay in its own space.

Fan loyalty program

A loyalty program that rewards what fans do for a brand away from the till, such as posting, bringing a friend, coming to an event or answering a quiz, instead of only rewarding purchases. It can run next to a shop’s points-per-purchase program. See Compare.

Brand advocacy program

A program that encourages existing customers to recommend a brand to people they know. Unlike an influencer campaign, it works with many ordinary customers rather than a few paid creators; FAN/IP rewards them with points and the rewards the brand chooses.

Active member

Someone who did something in the community this month. FAN/IP plans count active members, not sign-ups: Starter up to 150, Club up to 1,500, Season up to 10,000. See Pricing.

Mission

Something a brand asks its members to do, for points: post a photo or video, answer a question, check in at an event with a QR code, predict a score, bring a friend. Missions can be chained into a mission road with a reward at the end.

Points, levels and rewards

Members earn points for missions, move up the levels the brand names, and spend points on rewards the brand chooses: discounts, products, experiences or digital items. Each brand can rename its points, for example “petals” or “notes”.

Season

A competition with a start and a finish. Members climb a leaderboard by points over the season, and each one gets a personal recap at the end, made to be shared.

Team battle

Members pick a side and score points for it, for example North Stand against Riverside End. Those who played for the winning side share the reward.

Sponsor quest and sponsor report

A set of up to ten missions under a partner’s name, such as matchday photos or a QR check-in at the partner’s stand. The partner gets a private report: how many members took part, how many posts and views, and what that reach would have cost as ads at the CPM the club sets.

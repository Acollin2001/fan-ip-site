Pricing questions

How much does it cost?

Starter: €99 a month or €990 a year, no set-up fee, up to 150 active members. Club: €490 a month, set-up €1,250, up to 1,500 active members. Season: €1,290 a month, set-up €2,500, up to 10,000 active members. Signature: from €2,900 a month, set-up from €5,000: the group offer, several brands, clubs or countries on one contract, each space on quote. Prices are in euros, with no VAT charged (§ 19 UStG). Club, Season and Signature are billed monthly on a 6-month term. See pricing.

What is an active member?

A member who sent at least one mission in the last 90 days, approved or not. After 90 days without sending one, they count as inactive; their account stays.

What happens if we grow past our plan?

Members who already joined are never blocked. Starter caps active members at 150: once reached, new sign-ups join a waiting list, and you let them in as soon as a place is free (a member with no post in 90 days), or as soon as you move up a plan. Club's 1,500 and Season's 10,000 are soft limits: nobody waits and nobody is blocked. Above them two months in a row, the plan above applies from your next renewal (Signature at a price agreed with you), and we tell you before. No surprise invoices.

Can I try it for free?

Yes, on Starter: a 7-day free trial. You sign up online, pick your colours and fonts, and your space is ready. A card is asked at sign-up and nothing is charged during the trial; you can cancel before it ends. For Club, Season and Signature, book a demo.

Can I cancel?

Starter has no commitment: cancel at any time and it runs until the end of the period already paid. Club, Season and Signature are billed monthly on a 6-month term, renewed for another 6 months unless cancelled at least one month before the term ends, or month-to-month, without the 6-month term, for 20% more, agreed in writing, with one month’s notice.

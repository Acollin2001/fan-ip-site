This policy explains how personal data is handled when you visit https://fan-ip.com/ (the “site”) or write to us from it. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679, “GDPR”), the German Federal Data Protection Act (Bundesdatenschutzgesetz, BDSG) and, for anything stored on your device, § 25 of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG).

Who is responsible

The controller of your data is the provider of the site:

Name
Alexis Collin
Address
See the Imprint
Email
contact@fan-ip.com

What the site does not do

  • It sets no cookies and loads no advertising tool unless you accept them in the cookie banner (see Measurement and advertising). Visits are counted anonymously, without cookies (see Visit statistics).
  • It has no social-media plugins, and it never shares your data for advertising without your consent.
  • It sends no request to a third party: pages, styles, scripts and fonts are all served from the site’s own address. The exceptions are the anonymous visit statistics (see Visit statistics) and the booking calendar on the Book a demo page, only once you click to show it (see Booking a demo).
  • It has no forms and no accounts of its own: the only place you type anything is Google’s booking calendar, if you choose to use it.

Data processed anyway

Technical logs kept by the host

Like any website, the site is delivered by a server, and your browser has to send it some technical data to receive the pages. The host, GitHub, states that it logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes.

Data
IP address, date and time of the request, page requested, browser identification string.
Purpose
Deliver the site and keep it secure.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest in running a working, secure website (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Retention
Set by the host; see GitHub’s privacy statement.

We do not use these logs to measure audience or to profile anyone.

Emails you send us

When you write to us, for example through “Book a demo”, we receive your message and the data it carries.

Data
Your email address, your name and anything you choose to write, such as your company or role.
Purpose
Answer you and organise a demo.
Legal basis
Steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, beyond that, our legitimate interest in answering business enquiries (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Retention
Until your enquiry and any follow-up are settled; then deleted. Where an email is a business letter that German commercial or tax law requires us to keep (§ 257 HGB, § 147 AO), we keep it for the statutory period, six years from the end of the calendar year in which it was received or sent, and use it for nothing else (Article 6(1)(c) GDPR).

Visit statistics (anonymous, without cookies)

To see which pages are useful and where visitors stop on the way to booking a demo, the site counts visits with PostHog (PostHog Inc.), on its EU servers in Frankfurt, Germany. It uses no cookies and stores nothing on your device.

Data
Pages visited, the site you came from, clicks on a few buttons (Book a demo, Show available times, the email link, Start my free trial, the choice of world or demo, the first use of a calculator), browser type, screen size and approximate country. Your IP address is used only to compute an anonymous code that changes every day, and is not stored.
Purpose
Understand how the site is used and improve it. No profiles, no advertising, no session recordings.
Legal basis
Legitimate interest in audience measurement that does not identify you (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).
Retention
Up to one year, then deleted.
Provider
PostHog processes this data on our behalf under a data processing agreement (Article 28 GDPR).
Your choice
If your browser sends a “Do Not Track” signal, nothing is counted. You can also object at any time by writing to us (see Your rights).

Booking a demo

The Book a demo page uses an appointment schedule provided by Google Calendar (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland). Nothing is loaded from Google until you click “Show available times” or open the booking page on Google.

When you show the calendar
Your browser connects to Google, which receives your IP address and technical data about your device, and may use cookies or similar technologies under its own policy. Legal basis: your consent, given by the click (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG). If you do not click, nothing reaches Google.
When you book
Your name, email address, the emails of any colleagues you add and the time you choose are used to organise the demo: Google creates the meeting in Alexis Collin’s calendar, with a Google Meet link, and sends the invitations. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before a possible contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
How long
The calendar event is deleted once the demo and its follow-up are settled, unless it becomes part of business correspondence that must be kept (up to 6 years, § 257 HGB, § 147 AO).
Transfers
Google may process this data in the United States. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. See Google’s privacy policy.

You can always book without Google: write to contact@fan-ip.com.

Linked TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts

This section applies to members of a brand’s fan program powered by FAN/IP. Linking a social account is optional. It uses each network’s official sign-in: you log in on TikTok, Instagram or Google, never on FAN/IP, and we never see your password.

What we read
Read-only: your account ID, username, display name, profile picture, follower or subscriber count and post count, and your own recent posts (link, caption, thumbnail, date, views, likes and comments counts).
What we never do
We never publish, comment, like, send messages or change anything on your account. We never read your private messages or who your followers are.
Why
To show the linked account on your member page; to check the posts you make for the program’s missions (that the video is yours and carries the requested hashtag) and work out your rewards; and to prevent fraud, with one account per network per member in each program.
Who is responsible
The brand running the program is the controller of this data. FAN/IP processes it on the brand’s behalf, as its processor.
Legal basis
Your consent, given when you link the account (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by unlinking the account; this does not affect what was done before.
Security
The access keys each network gives us are encrypted and kept only on our servers.
Retention
Everything is deleted when you unlink the account, leave the program or delete your member account. Audience figures are refreshed regularly, or deleted, as each network’s rules require. How to ask for deletion: see Data deletion.

Each network’s own terms

  • YouTube: by linking a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service, and the Google Privacy Policy applies. FAN/IP’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can remove FAN/IP’s access at any time from your Google account permissions.
  • TikTok: the TikTok Privacy Policy applies.
  • Instagram: the Meta privacy policy for Instagram applies. Only Instagram Business or Creator accounts can be linked.

Measurement and advertising (only with your consent)

The site can use the tools below to count visits and measure its ads. None of them loads before you choose in the cookie banner, and “Reject all” is always one click. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of every page; withdrawing does not affect what happened before.

Analytics
Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland): pages visited, time on site, device and approximate location, with a shortened IP address. Cookies kept up to 2 years.
Advertising
Google Ads (Google Ireland Limited), LinkedIn Insight Tag (LinkedIn Ireland Unlimited Company, Wilton Plaza, Wilton Place, Dublin 2, Ireland) and Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Limited, Merrion Road, Dublin 4, Ireland): whether you came from one of our ads, whether you clicked “Book a demo”, and, for LinkedIn and Meta, showing our ads again to past visitors. Cookies kept up to 2 years (Google), up to 1 year (LinkedIn) and up to 90 days (Meta). For the Meta Pixel, we and Meta are jointly responsible for collecting and sending the data (Art. 26 GDPR); Meta is responsible for what it does with it afterwards.
Legal basis
Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
Your choice
Kept in your browser (local storage, key “fanip-consent”) for 6 months, then asked again. Storing it is strictly necessary to respect it (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG).
Transfers
These providers may process data in the United States. Google LLC, Microsoft Corporation (LinkedIn) and Meta Platforms, Inc. are certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Who receives it

  • Alexis Collin, who handles demo requests in person.
  • Our technical providers, only for what they do for us: the host, GitHub, Inc. (technical logs), and our email provider, OVHcloud (OVH SAS, France) (the emails you send us).
  • PostHog Inc., for the anonymous visit statistics, stored in the EU (see Visit statistics).
  • Google, if you use the booking calendar (see Booking a demo).

We do not sell your data, rent it out or share it for advertising.

Transfers outside the EU

The host, GitHub, Inc., is a company based in the United States. The technical data described above is therefore processed in the United States, under the conditions GitHub sets out in its own privacy statement.

Emails you send us are stored by OVHcloud in its data centres in France, within the EU. We do not transfer them outside the EU.

The anonymous visit statistics are stored by PostHog on its servers in Frankfurt, Germany, within the EU.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, and where each right applies, you can ask us for:

  • Access: to know whether we hold data about you, and to get a copy (Article 15).
  • Rectification: to correct data that is wrong or incomplete (Article 16).
  • Erasure: to have your data deleted (Article 17).
  • Restriction: to have its use suspended for a while (Article 18).
  • Portability: to receive the data you gave us in a reusable format (Article 20).

Right to object (Article 21 GDPR). Where we process your data on the basis of our legitimate interest, you can object at any time, on grounds relating to your particular situation. We then stop, unless we can show compelling legitimate grounds or need the data to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

How to exercise them

Write to contact@fan-ip.com, or by post to the address in the Imprint. We answer within one month. If we have reasonable doubts about who you are, we may ask for information to confirm your identity.

Complaints

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Article 77 GDPR), in particular in the EU country where you live or work, or where you think the infringement took place.

The authority responsible for us is the data protection authority of North Rhine-Westphalia: Landesbeauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit Nordrhein-Westfalen, Kavalleriestraße 2–4, 40213 Düsseldorf, Germany (www.ldi.nrw.de). The German authorities are listed by the Datenschutzkonferenz.

Cookies and your device

Without your consent, the site sets no cookies and stores nothing on your device, except your cookie choice itself once you make one. Measurement and advertising cookies are set only if you accept them (see Measurement and advertising). Google’s booking calendar is loaded only if you click to show it, and that click is your consent to what Google does on your device (see Booking a demo). The anonymous visit statistics use no cookies and store nothing on your device (see Visit statistics). What your browser keeps on its own to display the pages, such as its cache, is strictly necessary for the service you asked for (§ 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG). The cookie banner appears only when at least one of the tools above is switched on.

Changes

We may update this policy, for example when the site changes. The date at the top of the page shows the version in force.

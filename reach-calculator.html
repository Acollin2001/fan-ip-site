How we count

The same sum as in the product.

Media value is what the same views would have cost as paid posts: views ÷ 1,000 × your CPM.

  • Views, not followers

    We count views of each post, the figure the network shows the person who posted it. Follower counts make bigger numbers, and they aren’t what people actually saw.

  • Your CPM, not ours

    We start from a typical ad price for your niche: €7 per 1,000 views for sport, €5 for music and creators, €8 for brands. If your ads cost more or less, put in your own rate: the value moves with it, the views don’t.

  • What we leave out

    Shares, comments, saves, the friends who join after seeing a post, and anything they buy. Those come on top, and we don’t guess them here.

  • Then the real figures

    In FAN/IP, Post impact reads the real views of members’ posts from their linked accounts and prices them the same way, at your CPM. You can hold this estimate against what actually happens.

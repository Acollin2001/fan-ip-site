Figures as of 10 October 2026. Views, likes, comments and shares come from the networks, through the accounts members linked. Posts that were not approved are left out.

Media value: what the same views would have cost as paid posts, at €7 per 1,000 views, the CPM the club sets. Potential reach is the followers of the accounts that posted; it is shown next to the views and never used to compute media value.

About this example: the totals, the missions and the networks are the Weirbank Motors figures in the Aldermoor demo. The demo has no figures week by week, so the weekly split here is spread from each mission’s start date and adds up to the same totals.

Sponsor quests and this report come with the Club, Season and Signature plans. The club sets the CPM; change it and every value follows, while the views stay the same.

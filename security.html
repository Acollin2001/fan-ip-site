Where the data is

Your members’ data, their sign-in and the files they upload are hosted by Supabase in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, in the European Union. The app itself runs and is delivered through Cloudflare’s network. Source: DPA, Annex 3.

Who is responsible for what

You are the controller of your members’ data; FAN/IP processes it on your behalf, under a data processing agreement (Art. 28 GDPR) that is part of your contract. You choose who in your team has access and with which role. Source: DPA, Articles 1 and 4.3.

The data processing agreement (DPA)

It is part of your contract; online, you accept it with the terms before paying. Read it first: DPA in English or in German (the German version prevails). It lists what is processed, why, for how long, the security measures and the sub-processors.

When a fan links TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, those networks’ official sign-in is used; they act on their own account, not as sub-processors.

How the data is protected

Each brand’s data is kept apart by the database itself: every record carries its space, and access rules stop anyone, team member or fan, from reading another brand’s data. Roles (member, moderator, administrator, owner) are checked in the database. Source: DPA, Annex 2.

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest by the host; the keys to fans’ linked social accounts are encrypted once more. Uploaded files are private and served through links that expire after 5 minutes. Sensitive admin actions (roles, points, deletions, settings) are logged. Source: DPA, Annex 2.

Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app is required for every administrator, and for moderators once they set it up; it is then asked at every sign-in. Source: DPA, Annex 2, which also lists the measures being put in place.

Fans’ own data

Fans download their data and ask for its deletion from their profile; your team has a page to handle deletion requests. When a member is deleted, their profile, answers, post links, addresses, messages and friend connections go; the points history stays without any link to the person. Source: DPA, Article 7.2 and Annex 2. On this site: Data deletion.

If something goes wrong

FAN/IP tells you of a personal data breach without undue delay, and within 48 hours at the latest, even before the analysis is finished, and helps you notify your authority. Source: DPA, Article 8.

When the contract ends

You choose to get your data back, then it is deleted within 30 days of the end of the export period; backups follow within 30 more days. Deletion is confirmed in writing on request. Source: DPA, Article 12.

This website

fan-ip.com sets no cookies and loads no analytics or advertising tools. Source: Privacy policy.

Contact and authority

Data protection questions: contact@fan-ip.com. FAN/IP has no data protection officer, as none is required (§ 38 BDSG). The supervisory authority is the LDI NRW, the data protection authority of North Rhine-Westphalia. Source: DPA, Annex 4.

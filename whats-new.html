What’s new

What went live in FAN/IP, month by month, in plain words. Every entry is live today, with the day it went live; nothing here is a promise.

Last updated: 12 October 2026

On this page

  1. October 2026
  2. September 2026

The live demos show a few screens from our roadmap; something appears on this page only once it is live in the app. For everything FAN/IP does, see Platform and Pricing.

October 2026

Perks unlocked by level

8 October 2026

Give members an extra when they reach a level, or after a number of approved posts: automatically, or once your team approves it. Members find each perk on their perks page, with what to do next: a link, a code or a place to go.

Sign-up that starts from your world

6 October 2026

At sign-up you say whether you are a brand, a club or team, or an artist or creator, and your space starts with words and six ready-made missions to match. The first is live at once; the others wait as drafts.

Leaderboard and season wrap on Starter

6 October 2026

The season leaderboard and each member’s end-of-season recap are now part of Starter.

Where new members come from

6 October 2026

Results shows how each new member arrived: a member’s referral link or QR code, a shared mission or post, a check-in QR code, or an invite code.

Permission to repost, and your best posts

6 October 2026

A mission can ask members for the right to repost their post on your own channels; the box is optional and the answer is kept with the post. A best-posts page sorts approved posts by views or likes and shows which ones you may repost.

Bonus points at view milestones

6 October 2026

A mission can pay extra points when a post passes the numbers of views you choose.

Winners and shipping in one list

6 October 2026

Shop orders, season rewards, giveaway winners and sponsor quest prizes in a single list, each marked to do, sent or delivered, with the carrier and tracking number.

Digital rewards, delivered at once

6 October 2026

A reward can be a file or a link: members get it in the app the moment they redeem it, with nothing for your team to send.

Giveaways with an automatic draw

6 October 2026

From Club: winners are drawn automatically among members with an approved post during the giveaway, and the draw is recorded.

A link to your own shop

6 October 2026

An “Our shop” button sends members to your online shop, on every plan, and Results counts the clicks.

Two more live demos and the reach calculator

5 October 2026

On this site: Ilsa Morrow, an artist on tour, and Maison Aube, a beauty brand, next to Aldermoor; and the reach calculator, to estimate what your members’ posts could be worth.

September 2026

The app is live

30 September 2026

FAN/IP runs at app.fan-ip.com, with each brand’s space at its own address. Members’ data is hosted in Frankfurt, in the European Union. Details on Security and GDPR.

Starter sign-up online

30 September 2026

The Starter button on this site opens the sign-up at app.fan-ip.com/start: create your space yourself, without a call.

This website

29 September 2026

fan-ip.com went live, with plans and prices and a way to book a demo.
